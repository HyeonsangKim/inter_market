## 🌏 Overview

Inter Agora is a community-driven platform designed specifically for foreign residents in Korea, combining the convenience of local marketplace transactions with community engagement features. Think of it as a specialized version of Karrot Market (당근마켓) that caters to the unique needs of the international community in Korea.

### Key Features

🏠 **Location-Based Services**

- Neighborhood-focused community and marketplace
- Currently available in Seoul and Busan (More regions coming soon)
- Find people and items in your local area

💬 **Real-time Chat**

- Direct messaging for price negotiations
- Arrange meetups for item exchanges
- Discuss transaction details securely

🛍️ **Marketplace**

- Buy, sell, or exchange items
- Post items with detailed descriptions and images
- Filter items by location and category

👥 **Community Features**

- Share local information and tips
- Connect with neighbors
- Post and respond to community announcements

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Animation:** Framer Motion
- **UI Components:** React Icons, Heroicons
- **Type Safety:** TypeScript
- **Routing:** Next.js App Router with nested layouts

## 🌟 Unique Selling Points

- **Language Support**: Multilingual interface (English, Korean)
- **Foreign-Friendly**: Designed with foreign residents' needs in mind
- **Local Integration**: Helps international residents connect with their local community
- **Safe Transactions**: In-person transaction focused for added security
- **Community Building**: Emphasis on creating lasting connections in your neighborhood
